How to interpret this data

MAE is the average absolute difference between predicted and actual statistics, calculated on settled player-games only. Error colors represent size of prediction error, not betting confidence. Green does not establish a profitable betting edge. ESPN injury-feed entries are unofficial, may be stale, and match names only. “Not listed” does not verify a player will play. All forecasts remain unverified candidates, not wagering recommendations.

Player Availability & News ESPN injuries + Yahoo Sports headlines

Injury labels are unofficial reporting signals, not confirmed active/inactive decisions. Headlines are links for review, not verified lineup changes. RotoWire automatic collection is not enabled.
