How accuracy is calculated

A prediction is scored only after the game is complete and a matching player box score is available. Mean absolute error (MAE) is the average of |predicted − actual| over settled player-games. Green means error ≤2, amber ≤5, red >5 for the chosen statistic; these are display thresholds, not wagering confidence bands.

All projections are unverified model candidates. Injury availability, current sportsbook prices, and model calibration have not been verified. These are not betting recommendations.
